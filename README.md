Biochar is Carbon Negative, meaning it sequesters carbon.  Good biochar will trap carbon for 100+ years.  It is cheap and easy to make and it has been around since the dawn of time.  Literally.  Trees already take co2 out of the air, we just need to hang on to it.  Without pyrolysis and transformation into biochar, the carbon will biodegrade and return to the atmosphere.  By converting carbon in organic material into biochar we trap the carbon from going back into the atmosphere.  Also the byproduct is hydrogen which can be used for clean energy production.
